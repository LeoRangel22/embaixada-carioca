05 FAQ

Everything about the award-winning feijoada.

What is the relationship between Embaixada Carioca and Academia da Cachaça?+

Embaixada Carioca serves the feijoada from Academia da Cachaça through an exclusive partnership. Academia da Cachaça is a carioca gastronomic institution with over 40 years of tradition, elected by the Veja Rio Comer & Beber award as the best feijoada in Rio and consecrated by Prazeres da Mesa magazine as the best in Brazil. Its locations are in Leblon (the historic original) and Barra da Tijuca (the Cachaça Museum).

Is feijoada served every day?+

Yes. The award-winning feijoada at Embaixada Carioca is served every day, from 11:30am to 5pm — unlike the Brazilian tradition of serving it only on Saturdays. It is the only feijoada with a panoramic view of Sugarloaf Mountain, at 227 meters altitude at Morro da Urca.

What is the best feijoada in Rio de Janeiro?+

The Academia da Cachaça feijoada, served at Embaixada Carioca, was awarded the best feijoada in Brazil by Revista Prazeres da Mesa. It is prepared with dried beef, pork ribs, pork loin, sausage and smoked sausage in a clay pot.

How much does the feijoada cost?+

The Academia da Cachaça feijoada costs R$ 189.70 and serves 2 people. It includes dried beef, pork ribs, pork loin, sausage and smoked sausage, served with rice, collard greens, cassava flour, orange slices and the house honey.

Do I need to buy a cable car ticket?+

No, you do not need to buy a cable car ticket to access the restaurant. The entrance is at Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca, Rio de Janeiro. The cable car takes you to Morro da Urca (1st stop), where Embaixada Carioca is located.

Should I make a reservation?+

We recommend booking in advance, especially on weekends and holidays. Reservations can be made via the official website (Tagme) or via WhatsApp +55 21 96683-7556.
