What guests say about Embaixada Carioca.

Visitors highlight the view of Sugarloaf Mountain, the Brazilian lunch, the award-winning feijoada served in partnership with Academia da Cachaça, breakfast in the park and the relaxed Carioca service.

Why do tourists choose Embaixada Carioca?

Embaixada Carioca is located at Morro da Urca, inside Parque Bondinho Pão de Açúcar. It is a practical stop for breakfast, lunch, caipirinhas and snacks during the cable car visit, with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain.

View

Guests consistently mention the panoramic setting and the rare position inside the park.

Brazilian food

Picanha, feijoada, snacks and caipirinhas are the main reasons for choosing the restaurant during the visit.

Convenience

The restaurant works for tourists who want to eat without leaving the Morro da Urca experience.
