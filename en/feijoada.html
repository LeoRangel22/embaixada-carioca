05 FAQ

Everything about the award-winning feijoada.

What is the relationship between Embaixada Carioca and Academia da Cachaça?+

Embaixada Carioca serves the feijoada from Academia da Cachaça through an exclusive partnership. Academia da Cachaça is a carioca gastronomic institution with over 40 years of tradition, elected by the Veja Rio Comer & Beber award as the best feijoada in Rio and consecrated by Veja Rio 2025/2026 magazine as the best in Brazil. Its locations are in Leblon (the historic original) and Barra da Tijuca (the Cachaça Museum).

Is feijoada served every day?+

Yes. The award-winning feijoada at Embaixada Carioca is served every day, from 11:30 AM to 5:00 PM — unlike the Brazilian tradition of serving it only on Saturdays. It is the only feijoada with a panoramic view of Sugarloaf Mountain, at 227 meters altitude at Urca Hill.

What is the best feijoada in Rio de Janeiro?+

The Academia da Cachaça feijoada, served at Embaixada Carioca, was awarded the Award-winning feijoada from Academia da Cachaça by Revista Veja Rio 2025/2026. It is prepared with dried beef, pork ribs, pork loin, sausage and smoked sausage in a clay pot.

How much does the feijoada cost?+

The Academia da Cachaça feijoada costs R$ 189.70 and serves 2 people. It includes dried beef, pork ribs, pork loin, sausage and smoked sausage, served with rice, collard greens, cassava flour, orange slices and the house honey.

Do I need to buy a cable car ticket?+

No, you do not need to buy a cable car ticket to access the restaurant. The entrance is at Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca, Rio de Janeiro. The cable car takes you to Urca Hill (1st stop), where Embaixada Carioca is located.

Should I make a reservation?+

We recommend booking in advance, especially on weekends and holidays. Reservations can be made via the official website (Tagme) or via WhatsApp +55 21 96683-7556.

Frequently asked questions

Is feijoada served every day?

Feijoada is one of Embaixada Carioca’s signature Brazilian dishes. Availability may vary by day, so it is best to confirm when booking or arriving.

Is feijoada a good choice for visitors?

Yes. For travelers looking for a classic Brazilian dish during a Sugarloaf visit, feijoada offers a strong sense of Rio’s food culture with a view from Urca Hill.

Do I need a ticket to eat feijoada?

Yes, if you access the restaurant by cable car. Embaixada Carioca is inside Sugarloaf Cable Car Park, at Urca Hill. The trail is the free alternative when open.

How many people does it serve?

Portions may vary according to the menu version and appetite. Ask the team when booking or visiting to choose the best option.

Does it pair with caipirinha?

Yes. Caipirinha is one of the house icons and pairs naturally with feijoada, snacks and Brazilian dishes.

Does the restaurant have a view?

Yes. The restaurant is located at Urca Hill, with views of Sugarloaf Mountain and Guanabara Bay.

Should I reserve?

Reservations are recommended on weekends, holidays and high-traffic days at the park.

Is feijoada on the online menu?

The online menu includes the main house options. Some items may vary by availability, so confirm on the day or through the reservation channel.
