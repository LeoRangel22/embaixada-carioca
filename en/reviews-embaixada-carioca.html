Questions about reviews

What is Embaixada Carioca's rating on Google?

Embaixada Carioca has a 4.8-star rating on Google with over 8,600 reviews. It is consistently one of the highest-rated restaurants in Rio de Janeiro.

Does Embaixada Carioca have TripAdvisor reviews?

Yes, Embaixada Carioca has an "Excellent" rating on TripAdvisor, with hundreds of reviews from tourists from around the world who visited Urca Hill.

Is the feijoada at Embaixada Carioca really award-winning?

Yes! Embaixada Carioca serves the feijoada from Academia da Cachaça, voted one of the best in Rio de Janeiro by Veja Rio magazine (Comer & Beber 2025 and 2026). It is served every day at lunch, from 12pm to 5pm.
