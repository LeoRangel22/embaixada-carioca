The breakfast with the most beautiful view in Brazil

When it comes to breakfast with a view in Rio de Janeiro, there is one option that has no comparison: Embaixada Carioca, at the top of Urca Hill. While other restaurants offer views of the seafront or the ocean, Embaixada Carioca offers something unique in the world — breakfast facing Sugarloaf Mountain, 227 metres above sea level.

Imagine waking up early, riding the cable car as the sun is still rising, and sitting down at a table with the silhouette of Sugarloaf Mountain lit by the golden morning light. That is the experience Embaixada Carioca offers every day — and one that no other breakfast spot in Rio, or in Brazil, can replicate.

Embaixada Carioca — The Only Breakfast with a View of Sugarloaf Mountain

Open every day from 8:30am to 11:30am, at the top of Urca Hill. Inside Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park, at the first cable car stop. Access via Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca, Rio de Janeiro.

Why breakfast at Urca Hill is unique

There are many beautiful breakfast spots in Rio de Janeiro — in luxury hotels in Ipanema, in restaurants with seafront views in Copacabana, in charming bistros in Jardim Botânico. But none of them offer what Embaixada Carioca has: a view directly facing Sugarloaf Mountain.

Sugarloaf Mountain is the most recognised symbol of Rio de Janeiro in the world. Seen from afar, from the seafront or from Corcovado, it is a beautiful landscape. But seen up close, from the top of Urca Hill, 227 metres above sea level, with the cable car passing alongside and Guanabara Bay in the background — it is an experience that turns any breakfast into a lifelong memory.

Furthermore, Embaixada Carioca is the only restaurant in Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain — which means you have exclusive access to this view before tourists start arriving en masse. At 8:30am, when breakfast begins, Urca Hill is peaceful, the light is golden and the experience is incomparable.

How to get to breakfast with a view at Sugarloaf Mountain

Embaixada Carioca is located at Urca Hill, inside Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park, at the first cable car stop. Getting there is straightforward:

  1. Arrive at Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park: Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca, Rio de Janeiro. By car, Uber, taxi or bus (line 107 or 512).
  2. Take the cable car: The journey takes about 3 minutes and already offers an incredible view of the city.
  3. Disembark at Urca Hill: Embaixada Carioca is on your left as you exit. Breakfast is served from 8:30am to 11:30am.
  4. Enjoy: Sit down facing Sugarloaf Mountain and live a unique experience in Rio de Janeiro.

Frequently asked questions about breakfast with a view in Rio

Where to have breakfast with a view in Rio de Janeiro?+

Embaixada Carioca, at Urca Hill, is the only breakfast spot with a direct view of Sugarloaf Mountain in Rio de Janeiro. Located 227 metres above sea level, inside Bondinho Pão de Açúcar Park, it is open every day from 8:30am to 11:30am.

What are the breakfast hours at Embaixada Carioca?+

Breakfast at Embaixada Carioca is served every day, from 8:30am to 11:30am, at the top of Urca Hill. After 11:30am, the restaurant serves lunch until 5pm, and sunset dining from 5pm to 9pm.

Do I need a cable car ticket to have breakfast?+

You do not need to buy a cable car ticket to access the restaurant. Access is via Av. Pasteur, 520, Urca, Rio de Janeiro. The cable car takes you to Urca Hill (1st stop), where Embaixada Carioca is located.

What is served at breakfast at Embaixada Carioca?+

Breakfast includes artisan breads, fresh tropical fruits, tapioca, eggs, cold cuts, cheeses, natural juices, coffee and teas — all with a panoramic view of Sugarloaf Mountain. A unique experience in Rio de Janeiro.

What is the best breakfast with an ocean view in Rio?+

Breakfast at Embaixada Carioca, at Urca Hill, offers the best view in Rio de Janeiro — facing Sugarloaf Mountain, with Guanabara Bay in the background, 227 metres above sea level. It is considered the breakfast with the most beautiful view in Brazil.

Can I combine breakfast with lunch?+

Yes. Breakfast runs until 11:30am and lunch starts at noon. You can spend the whole day at Embaixada Carioca — starting with breakfast at dawn and finishing with the sunset with a view of the sun setting behind Sugarloaf Mountain.
