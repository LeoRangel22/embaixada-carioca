04 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions about corporate events.

How does the Cable Car logistics work for guests?

We provide full coordination support with the Sugarloaf Cable Car Park team. Your guests will enjoy priority fast-track boarding directly to Urca Hill.

Does the venue have a generator and technical support?

Yes. The Park is equipped with high-capacity generators that support the restaurant, and our AV staff is available throughout your event.

Can we customize the event menu?

We work with fully customizable set menus to accommodate dietary restrictions (vegan, gluten-free options) and international guest profiles.

What is the curfew/time limit for evening events?

We operate within the Park's standard operating hours. Special late-night extensions can be arranged in advance with our sales team.
